# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Artemis is a personal, voice-first macOS assistant for its owner while they work across Terminal, browser, communications, and local apps. The primary workflow is hands-free coordination: understand the current context, carry out a safe action, and keep the user oriented without taking focus away from their work.

## Product Purpose

Artemis turns natural spoken or typed intent into deterministic, permission-aware local and connected actions. Success means the assistant remains useful offline, exposes its real state honestly, feels continuously present in full and floating modes, and never substitutes decorative telemetry for application truth.

## Brand Personality

Intelligent, composed, alive. Artemis should feel premium and technically capable without becoming theatrical, verbose, or game-like. Her voice and interface are concise, contextual, and quietly confident.

## Anti-references

- A generic chat assistant or white SaaS dashboard.
- A static sci-fi screenshot whose motion and telemetry are decorative.
- Excessive cyberpunk neon, video-game HUD clutter, or effects that compete with the current task.
- A miniature dashboard squeezed into the floating presence.
- Robotic system copy or invented metrics that imply activity which did not occur.

## Design Principles

1. Real state earns every visual response.
2. The intelligence core and current task lead; diagnostics recede.
3. Floating mode is Artemis distilled to one living object, not a status toolbar.
4. Preserve focus and continuity across full, pill, and background presentation modes.
5. Local-first behavior, permissions, and deterministic routing remain authoritative beneath every surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, keyboard- and screen-reader-compatible controls where web content is interactive, and a complete reduced-motion mode. State may use color as reinforcement but must remain legible through shape, text, or motion-independent cues. Never require animation to understand status or approve an action.
